Product Overview & Specifications

Meet the New TYPHON TERROR XVII PRO with an operating weight of 3,800 lb, this mini excavator delivers the stability, power, and durability required for demanding jobs. Featuring upgraded protection, refined hydraulics, and enhanced operator comfort, this machine is engineered to tackle tough conditions with confidence. Reinforced components and hydraulic protection help maximize uptime, while the redesigned operator station keeps you comfortable and productive throughout the day.

1. EPA-Certified Kubota Engine

Kubota D902 diesel engine: Producing 15.8 HP at 2200 RPM and up to 20.4 HP at 3600 RPM, the Japanese-built Kubota D902 is a compact vertical 3-cylinder, 4-cycle diesel engine engineered for smooth power delivery, outstanding fuel efficiency, and long-term durability. Trusted worldwide for its reliability.

EPA-certified performance: Built to meet strict EPA emissions standards, allowing you to work confidently while maintaining clean and efficient performance.

Extended runtime capacity: With a generous 4.76-gallon hydraulic oil reservoir and a 3.17-gallon fuel tank, the machine is built to stay productive longer, reducing downtime and ensuring reliable performance throughout demanding workdays.

2. Advanced Hydraulic System

High-pressure hydraulic system: Operating at 18 MPa (2,610.68 PSI), the hydraulic system provides strong and consistent power for digging and demanding attachment applications.

Precision pilot controls: Featuring a 1.82 GPM pilot flow, the system delivers smooth, responsive, and precise operation for grading, trenching, and detailed work.

Dual auxiliary hydraulic circuits: Built for superior versatility, the double-side auxiliary throttle valves provide uninterrupted hydraulic flow to powered attachments and the hydraulic thumb simultaneously. Whether drilling with an auger, breaking concrete, or handling materials, this 3,800lbs mini excavator keeps you productive without the need for setup changes.

Integrated hydraulic oil cooler: Helps prevent overheating during continuous operation, maintains consistent performance in hot climates, and extends hydraulic system life.

3. Superior Mobility & Reach

Precision offset boom swing: Offering 45° left swing and 55° right swing, the offset boom allows precise digging around walls, foundations, and confined spaces with minimal repositioning.

Smooth full 360° swing: Combined with a powerful 6.07 GPM main hydraulic flow, the continuously rotating upper structure motor provides effortless positioning, faster cycle times, and outstanding flexibility for digging, loading, and material handling from virtually any angle.

Confident travel speed: Traveling up to 1.30 MPH, the machine moves smoothly and confidently around the jobsite.

Sure-Grip rubber tracks: Built with wide 9.06-inch tracks, the undercarriage delivers excellent flotation, superior traction, and dependable stability across muddy, soft, and uneven terrain.

4. Exceptional Digging Performance

Powerful 14 kN digging force: Delivers the breakout power needed to penetrate hard soil, compacted ground, and roots with ease, helping complete demanding tasks more efficiently.

All-in-One work package: Equipped with a 16.54-inch bucket, 0.04 m³ bucket capacity, 86.50-inch maximum digging depth, standard dozer blade, and hydraulic thumb clip, the TERROR XVII PRO is ready to dig, grade, grab, and clean up with exceptional versatility while moving more material in fewer passes.

5. Premium-Modern Design & Operator Comfort

Modern Blue exterior design: Clean lines and a vibrant blue finish give this machine a contemporary look that stands out wherever it works.

Ergonomic operator seat: Featuring a premium headrest and armrests to provide exceptional support and comfort during extended working hours.

Spacious operator station: With an interior height of 41.34 inches and width of 20.08 inches, the roomy cabin helps reduce fatigue and keeps operators productive throughout long shifts.

Centrally located service access: Both the diesel fuel tank and hydraulic oil reservoir are conveniently positioned in one location, allowing quick refilling, easier servicing, and less downtime throughout the workday.

Armored hydraulic hoses and protected valves: Heavy-duty armored hoses and shielded hydraulic valves are designed to resist abrasion, impacts, and harsh working conditions. Together, they help prevent leaks, minimize downtime, and maximize long-term reliability in demanding applications.
