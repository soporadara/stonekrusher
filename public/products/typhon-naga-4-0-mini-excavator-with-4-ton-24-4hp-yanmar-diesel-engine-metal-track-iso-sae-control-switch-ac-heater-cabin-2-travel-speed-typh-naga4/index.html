Product Overview & Specifications

The TYPHON NAGA 4.0 is a compact yet powerful mini excavator designed for small and urban projects where space and performance matter. Its zero-tail swing, 360° rotation, and 860 mm tail swing radius allow safe, efficient operation in confined job sites, while the stable undercarriage, 30° grade ability, and 55° left/right boom swing deliver smooth, precise control for daily excavation work. Despite its compact size, the NAGA 4.0 provides exceptional digging strength with a 30.4 kN bucket force and 18.2 kN arm force, making it ideal for trenching, foundations, and tough soil conditions. Powered by the reliable YANMAR 3TNV88F engine, it combines strong performance, fuel efficiency, and compliance with EPA Tier 4 Final and EU Stage V standards. The operator-friendly cabin features ISO/SAE selectable controls, a 7-inch touchscreen display for machine monitoring and settings, an adjustable suspension seat, and LED work lights, ensuring comfort, precision, and productivity throughout long working hours.

TYPHON NAGA 4.0 Key Feature:


1. Precision in Tight Spaces:

Designed for small projects, this compact machine delivers strong performance in tight and urban job sites. With a zero-tail swing design, 360° rotation, and an 860 mm tail swing radius, it allows safe and efficient operation in confined spaces. Measuring 4,880 mm in overall length, 1,740 mm in overall width, and 2,550 mm in overall height (to top of cap), it offers an ideal balance of size and capability. A 2,155 mm track length, 1,440 mm track gauge, and 30° grade ability ensure stability on uneven terrain. The TYPHON NAGA 4.0 series delivers raw power that defies its size, engineered to penetrate the toughest terrain. This machine boasts a massive 30.4 KN bucket digging force and an 18.2 KN arm digging force, ensuring it cuts through compacted clay or rocky soil with ease. Featuring a high-capacity 0.12 m³ bucket capacity with a 650mm bucket width, it is optimized for rapid material handling and heavy-duty trenching. 3. Heavily Power:

Under the hood of this 4-ton powerhouse sits the world-renowned YANMAR 3TNV88F diesel engine. This three-cylinder, direct-injection engine delivers smooth, reliable torque and generates 18.2 kW (24.4 hp) at 2200 rpm, while meeting strict EPA Tier 4 Final and Europe Stage V emission standards. With a 1.642 L displacement, it provides the high-output performance needed for grueling daily operations without sacrificing fuel efficiency. Built for endurance, the machine features a large 50 L fuel tank and a 40 L hydraulic oil tank, minimizing downtime, while the advanced cooling system and 6.7 L engine oil capacity ensure peak performance under heavy load. Whether traversing the site at Two Travel speed 2.4-4.4 km/h or performing Low-High precision excavation, this Yanmar-powered system delivers the relentless strength, consistent hydraulic flow, efficiency, and compliance required for modern industrial projects.

TYPHON NAGA 4.0 - Key Performance Summary:

  • The ISO / SAE control selection allows the operator to choose the joystick pattern that matches their operating experience.
    • ISO pattern (excavator standard): Left joystick controls Stick/Dipper (In/Out) and Swing (Left/Right), Right joystick controls Boom (Up/Down) and Bucket (Curl/Dump).
    • SAE pattern (backhoe pattern): Left joystick controls Boom (Up/Down) and Swing (Left/Right), Right joystick controls Stick/Dipper (In/Out) and Bucket (Curl/Dump).
  • The YANMAR 3TNV88F diesel engine is a compact, 3-cylinder, water-cooled engine known for its reliability, fuel efficiency, and compliance with strict emissions regulations like EPA Final Tier 4 and EU Stage V.
  • Zero Tail Swing: Rear structure stays within track width during rotation, allowing safe operation in confined and urban spaces.
  • Left & Right Boom Swing (55° / 55°): Allows offset digging along walls, foundations, and road edges without repositioning the machine.
  • 360° Upper Structure Swing: Full rotation improves digging and loading efficiency while reducing machine movement.
  • Two-Speed Travel: Low speed 2.4 km/h provides high torque for digging and slopes high speed 4.4 km/h enables faster site travel.
  • Plunger Pump: Standard auxiliary 96.8 L/min Hydraulic flow circuit supports breakers and multiple attachments.
  • Smart Digital Dashboard: 7-inch touchscreen displays engine data, hydraulic status, working hours, fault codes, maintenance reminders, and control pattern selection.
  • Three-way stop valve: These valves are used to add a hydraulic function, such as an auger or thumb clip attachment, by rerouting the flow from an existing hydraulic line.
  • Large Cabin Operation: Enclosed cabin with air conditioning and heater, openable front glass window for ventilation and communication.
  • Metal Tracks: highly durable, sturdy machines designed for harsh conditions like demolition and rocky terrain, offering better traction and longevity than their rubber-tracked counterparts.
