Product Overview & Specifications

The TYPHON Fury 20 Road Roller delivers consistent, effective compaction across asphalt, soil, and gravel. Engineered for road building and landscaping, its sturdy design offers a small footprint for effortless maneuvering. This roller combines powerful hydraulic drive and advanced vibration technology to improve efficiency and reduce the number of passes required. With a high climbing ability and intuitive veer steering, it ensures safe, precise operation on any terrain. TYPHON Fury 20 Key Features:

1. Wide Applications:

Engineered for versatility and reliable performance, this tandem roller is ideal for producing smooth, professional-grade surfaces. It is well-suited for highways, municipal roads, residential driveways, parking lots, and industrial construction sites. Featuring a 1000 mm drum width and 610 mm drum diameter, along with compact dimensions of 2300 mm overall length, 1120 mm total width, and 1650 mm height (excluding impact beam), it delivers excellent maneuverability and strong compaction power, even in confined working areas.

2. Effective Compaction:

Performance achieves smooth, even results on asphalt, soil, and gravel with this high-efficiency compactor. It combines a powerful hydraulic two-wheel drive with precision front drum hydraulic vibration to ensure maximum density on every pass. Engineered for versatility, it features 30% climbing capability on inclines, a 75 Hz vibration frequency, and a 0.5 mm nominal amplitude. With a consistent 35 kN excitation force, it delivers the professional-grade power needed for demanding job sites.

3. Refined Compact Strength:

This model features a sturdy, small-footprint design that delivers superior maneuverability without sacrificing power. It is powered by a 23 hp Briggs & Stratton engine, supporting a variable speed range of 0–12 km/h and an efficient fuel consumption rate of 240 g/kWh. With an operating weight of 2 Ton and a large 36L tank capacity, it provides the stability and endurance required for extended operations in tight or demanding job sites.

4. User-Friendly Controls:

Engineered for safe and precise operation, this unit features an intuitive control layout with hydraulic steering for smooth, effortless maneuvering. The advanced electronically controlled pressure water spray system provides efficient, even drum sprinkling while minimizing water waste. One-key vibration activation with an electromagnetic clutch front drum vibration for uniform surface compaction, reliable compaction control, and the comfortable, ergonomic operator seat reduces fatigue during extended use, allowing full focus on achieving a flawless finish.
