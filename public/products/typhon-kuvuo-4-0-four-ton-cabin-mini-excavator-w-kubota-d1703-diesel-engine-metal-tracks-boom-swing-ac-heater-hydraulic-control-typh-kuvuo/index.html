Product Overview & Specifications

TYPHON KUVUO 4.0 mini excavator is a versatile and reliable machine designed to work efficiently in tight spaces. Its compact size and system allow smooth maneuvering through narrow areas, making it ideal for construction sites, farms, and urban projects. Built for strength and consistent performance, it handles digging, trenching, landscaping, and material handling with ease. The addition of boom swing enhances flexibility, allowing operators to work close to walls, corners, and obstacles without repositioning the machine. The comfortable AC/heater cabin, equipped with LED lights and climate control, ensures operators can work safely and efficiently, completing tasks quickly while maintaining precision and control.

TYPHON KUVUO 4.0 Key Features:

1. Ideal for Tight-Space Tasks:

The TYPHON KUVUO 4.0 is designed to allow operators to confidently navigate narrow zones and busy job sites, ensuring maximum efficiency in constrained work spaces. With a total length of 5029 mm, a width of only 1720 mm, and a height of 2580 mm , it easily fits where larger equipment cannot. The 1554 mm upper width and 2150 mm track length provide balanced stability, while the 300 mm standard track shoe and boom swing, offer dependable ground grip and flexible movement.

Ideal for building sites, farms, and material handling in tight urban or residential areas.

2. One Machine, So Many Uses:

This 4 Ton Mini excavator is designed for general-purpose tasks such as trenching and foundation preparation, as well as landscaping and material handling. With an 18.2 kN boom force and 30.4 kN bucket force, along with a digging depth of 3106 mm, this mighty beast provides dependable power for tough jobs in construction sites, farms, and tight-space projects. Equipped with a Multi-Way Valve, it can also operate attachments like an auger, hydraulic thumb clip, and hydraulic breaker hammer, expanding its capabilities for drilling, grappling, or breaking tasks. (Attachments are compatible but not included.)

3. Strong but Compact Design:

Powered by a reliable 25 HP Kubota D1703 engine running at 2200 RPM, this mini excavator delivers strong performance in a compact 4-ton operating weight. Its low speed of 2.4 km/h and high speed of 4.2 km/h provide smooth movement, while the hydraulic oil cooler enables the system to function effectively throughout extended works. With a 55 L hydraulic oil tank and a 50 L fuel tank, it allows you to work non-stop for hours. The piston pump and load-sensing system deliver rapid, efficient hydraulic power for demanding jobs.

4. Easy Control:

The TYPHON KUVUO 4.0 is designed for smooth, easy operation, with a multi-way valve and modern hydraulic systems for accurate movement. Its fully hydraulic control, Pilot control, and clear control panel simplify every operation for the operator. LED lighting improve vision, and the AC/Heater cabin provides year round comfort. With a system flow of 79.2 L/min, it provides rapid, responsive performance on every job.

5. Gear Included:

The TYPHON KUVUO 4.0 mini excavator machine includes 1 x regular digging excavator Bucket, Hydraulic Thump Clip, 1 x tool box, and a digital manual with complete instructions.
