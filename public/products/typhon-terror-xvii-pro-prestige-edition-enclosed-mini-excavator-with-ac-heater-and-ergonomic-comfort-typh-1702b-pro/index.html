TYPHON TERROR XVII PRO Prestige Edition – Enclosed Mini Excavator with AC, Heater & Ergonomic Comfort

TYPHON TERROR XVII PRO Prestige Edition – Enclosed Mini Excavator with AC, Heater & Ergonomic Comfort is designed for owners who want professional capability without unnecessary complexity. The machine offers clear controls, practical service access, a comfortable enclosed cab, and a standard equipment package that supports digging, grading, grabbing, and cleanup work from the first day.

Simple Power You Can Depend On

The Kubota D902 diesel engine is widely valued for dependable compact-equipment performance. Its smooth power delivery supports routine maintenance work as well as more demanding digging and material-handling tasks.

Controls That Feel Predictable

Pilot-operated hydraulics provide responsive movement without requiring harsh control input. The hydraulic system supports the boom, bucket, swing functions, hydraulic thumb, and additional powered attachments.

More Work with Less Repositioning

The offset boom allows the machine to work closer to walls, fences, foundations, and landscape features. Continuous upper rotation helps place material efficiently and keeps the workflow moving.

Useful Equipment Included

The standard bucket, dozer blade, and hydraulic thumb let owners dig, grade, backfill, grab logs or debris, and clean up material without immediately adding multiple accessories.

Comfort and Convenience

Heat, air conditioning, a supportive seat, grouped fill points, and an enclosed operator area make the excavator easier to use across different seasons and working conditions.
