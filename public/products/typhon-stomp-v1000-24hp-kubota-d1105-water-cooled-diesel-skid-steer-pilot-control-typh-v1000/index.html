Product Overview & Specifications

The TYPHON STOMP V1000 is a compact yet powerful 1.5-Ton skid steer loader designed for confined locations such as gardens, small building sites, farms, and urban projects. Its compact footprint allows it to operate effortlessly in areas where larger machines cannot, while its versatile attachment capability enables digging, lifting, drilling, leveling, breaking, and site cleanup with ease.

Intuitive controls and a comfortable operator area make operation simple and efficient. Bright LED work lights ensure excellent visibility in all weather and lighting conditions, while quick attachment changes keep productivity high. The STOMP V1000 is well suited for landscaping, residential construction, agricultural work, material handling, and warehouse applications.

TYPHON STOMP V1000 Skid Steer – Key Features:

1. Built for Narrow Spaces

The TYPHON Stomp V1000 is compact yet powerful, making it ideal for tight access areas such as gardens, orchards, greenhouses, tunnels, and small construction sites. With a 980 mm chassis width, 1000 mm bucket width, 3000 mm overall length, and 1530 mm height, it can easily access locations where larger machines are restricted.

A 0–1760 mm turning radius combined with 360° mobility allows smooth maneuvering in confined spaces. The low 24 kPa ground pressure, 78 mm ground clearance, and 30° climbing ability provide stable operation on soft soil, farmland, muddy construction sites, slopes, and uneven terrain, helping prevent sinking or surface damage. The maximum lifting height of 2852 mm supports efficient material handling and loading tasks.

2. Get Jobs Done

STOMP V1000 is engineered to handle real-world workloads with ease. It digs dirt, sand, and gravel, lifts and transports loads up to 1,720 lbs (780 kg rated operating capacity), levels and clears ground; drills holes, breaks through hard surfaces, and efficiently loads or unloads trucks and containers.

With quick-swap attachments and an auxiliary hydraulic flow of 33–48 L/min, the machine supports a wide range of hydraulic tools including augers, breakers, grapples, trenchers, and sweepers. Its load-sensing hydraulic system allows simultaneous operation of the boom and attachments, reducing cycle time and increasing job productivity by eliminating unnecessary interruptions.

3. Compact and Powerful

Designed to deliver strong performance in a compact package, the STOMP V1000 is powered by a Kubota D1105-E4 diesel engine producing 18.2 kW (24.4 hp) at 3,000 rpm; this 3-cylinder, water-cooled, 4-cycle engine meets EU Stage V / EPA Tier 4 Final emissions standards, combining power, fuel efficiency, and proven long-term reliability, while its 4.5 km/h travel speed ensures smooth, efficient movement across job sites with excellent control and stability, making it ideal for both tight spaces and demanding work environments.

With 18 MPa working pressure, and a load-sensing hydraulic system, the machine delivers stable, continuous power with minimal energy loss, even during heavy-load or long-duration operations. Its 1,500 kg operating weight and reinforced structure provide excellent stability and resistance to deformation during lifting, excavation, and loading tasks. A 20 L diesel fuel tank and 22 L hydraulic oil tank support extended workdays with reduced refueling downtime.

4. Comfortable Controls

The TYPHON Stomp V1000 features full hydraulic pilot controls for precise digging, lifting, and maneuvering. Light control force improves accuracy while significantly reducing operator fatigue, allowing both experienced and new operators to work confidently and efficiently. The operator’s standing platform is equipped with foam padding to reduce vibration and fatigue during extended working hours, improving overall comfort and control.

A clear operator panel displays fuel level, oil temperature, voltage, and hour meter for easy monitoring of machine status. Bright LED work lights provide excellent visibility for safe operation in low-light or night conditions.
