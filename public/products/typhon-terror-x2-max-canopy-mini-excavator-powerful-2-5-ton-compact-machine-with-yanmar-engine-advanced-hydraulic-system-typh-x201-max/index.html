Product Overview & Specifications

The TYPHON TERROR X2 MAX is a compact yet powerful mini excavator designed for versatility, efficiency, and reliable performance across construction, landscaping, and utility applications. Its compact size allows easy operation in confined spaces while maintaining strong digging power and stability. Equipped with advanced hydraulics, a durable engine, and operator-friendly controls, it ensures smooth handling and consistent productivity. The ergonomic design improves comfort during long working hours, while its robust construction delivers dependable performance for demanding job sites. Ideal for professionals seeking power, flexibility, and efficiency in one machine.

TYPHON TERROR X2 MAX Key Feature:

1. Ideal for Tight-Space Tasks:

Designed for tight-space operations, this compact machine combines power and precision with an operating weight of 2.5 Ton and dimensions of 4000 × 1350 × 2280 mm. A Danfoss swing motor delivers smooth 360° rotation at 10 r/min, while a 2027 mm minimum swing radius ensures excellent maneuverability. The boom swings 55° left and 45° right for flexible operation, and the telescopic track range (1060–1350 mm) provides both stability and easy access in confined job sites.

2. One Machine, So Many Uses:

The TYPHON TERROR X2 MAX delivers versatile performance for multiple job applications. Featuring a 0.07 m³ digging bucket capacity and 22 KN digging force, it handles excavation tasks efficiently. With a 400 kg boom lifting capacity, 2915 mm max digging depth, and 3530 mm max digging height, it ensures strong reach and productivity. Equipped with a hydraulic thumb clip, double-sided throttle valve, and 35° gradeability, it adapts easily to demanding worksites.

3. Strong but Compact Design:

Built for durability and efficiency, this machine is powered by a reliable Yanmar 3TNV88F engine delivering 23.2 HP at 2200 rpm to 24.4 HP at 2400 rpm for consistent performance. An oil-cooled hydraulic system ensures stable operation during demanding tasks. With a 16 L fuel tank and 22 L hydraulic oil tank capacity, it supports extended working hours. Equipped with a Danfoss traveling motor, it reaches speeds up to 3.4 km/h. Optimized hydraulic performance includes 35 L/min pump displacement, 26 L/min main system flow, and 13 L/min pilot flow for smooth, responsive control.

4. Easy Control:

Designed with operator comfort and simplicity in mind, this machine features ergonomic armrests and a comfortable seat for reduced fatigue during long working hours. A clear display panel provides essential operating information, while pilot controls ensure smooth and precise handling. LED work lights improve visibility in low-light conditions, and the durable 4-leg canopy offers added protection and safety on the job site.

5. Gear Included:

  • Comes with 1 x standard bucket, 1 x hydraulic oil cooler, and 1 x hydraulic thumb clip.
  • Includes 1 x toolbox and 1 x digital manual with comprehensive instructions.
