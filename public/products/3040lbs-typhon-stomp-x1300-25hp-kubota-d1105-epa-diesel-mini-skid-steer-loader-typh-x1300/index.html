  • Features premium components such as IMPRO motor, Taifeng plunger pump, and Lufthansa handle joystick system.

  • The STOMP X1300 is equipped with Piston Pump, which ensures an efficient hydraulic flow rate throughout the machine. Out of the three pumps, the two in front are used for controlling overall machine movement, while the one at the back is used for controlling actions (such as the arm and auxiliary equipment).
  • Meanwhile, two of the three valves are for controlling the left and right side drive motors. The third valve is under the mechanical control lever, which controls the actions (of the arm, hydraulic cylinders, and auxiliary equipment).
  • The hydraulic oil radiator is placed separate from the engine compartment, so the hydraulic oil temperature is not affected by the heat generated by the engine.
