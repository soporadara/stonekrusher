Why Battery Capacity Must Match Scissor Lift Duty Cycle

Published on September 5, 2026 by KONSTRUCTZ Machinery Team

An electric scissor lift can carry the right battery chemistry, sit fully charged at the start of a shift, and still leave a crew stranded at height by mid-afternoon if battery capacity is mismatched to duty cycle. Continuous elevation cycles, drive time across slab floors, and heavy platform loads draw sustained current from the battery pack.

Deep-cycle flooded lead-acid and AGM batteries provide reliable low-cost energy but require regular maintenance and full charging cycles. AGM and AGM-sealed batteries eliminate watering requirements, while Lithium-ion batteries support opportunity charging during lunch breaks without memory degradation.

Selecting a machine with adequate Amp-Hour (Ah) capacity ensures that hydraulic lift pumps and electric drive motors receive stable voltage throughout an 8 to 10 hour shift, preserving lifting speed and drive control.
